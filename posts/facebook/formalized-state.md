ONE STRUCTURE UNDER EVERY GOVERNMENT

The six-pillar framework identifies an invariant structure of human governance, supported by historical observation and given precise expression through mathematics.

Part VII of our Beast System series ties the six pillars (resources, rule making, interpretation, belief, enforcement, production) into one formal object called the Formalized State, and proves four things about it.

REPRESENTATION: any governance system meeting the axioms matches this structure, with ideology and procedure set aside.

NECESSITY: six basic facts of collective life (scarcity, plurality, generality, coordination, defection, maintenance) force the six functions.

MINIMALITY: drop or merge any pillar and you blur systems that really differ.

UNIQUENESS: it is the coarsest description that keeps what matters, with exactly six dimensions.

The key point: the structure is universal, but capture is contingent. Systems that fit every axiom can be concentrated or dispersed, tightly linked or loosely linked. Having the structure does not mean being captured.

'Beast System' is a typological reading of Revelation 13, used here as a name for a structure and never for a religion, people, regime, country, party or person. Capitalist, communist, democratic socialist, Islamic, historical Catholic monarchical and secular democratic systems are treated evenly.

The ideology changes. The institutional functions remain.
