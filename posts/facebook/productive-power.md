WHO PRODUCES WHAT THE SYSTEM LIVES ON?

Every government, church, army and court eats. Somebody has to grow the food, make the goods and raise the next generation of workers.

Part VI of our Beast System series proves that any lasting group must produce at a positive rate and must reproduce its producers through care labor. Rents, tribute, migration or automation can move production around, but they do not remove it.

That gives producers a quiet kind of leverage. If outside revenue does not cover what the institutions cost to run, a collective withdrawal of labor, taxes or trade threatens the whole arrangement.

In the bargaining model, producers do better with a strong outside option and organization, and worse as control over who gets to participate in production becomes more concentrated. The effect is linear. Once rents cover maintenance, the withdrawal leverage disappears.

Every tool that squeezes producers sits in another pillar, so capture of production never starts inside it.

The ideology changes. The institutional functions remain.
