1/5 Who shapes the beliefs cooperation depends on? Part IV of the Beast System series starts with coordination: a group needs its operative rules to be common knowledge, so every persistent collective has a recognition function.

2/5 That is a claim about a function, not an institution, and not a claim that belief in the rightness of rules is necessary. Egalitarian societies do it through talk, ridicule, ritual and kinship idiom, with no designated office.

3/5 A Bayesian endorsement model gives a clean result: a captured authority's endorsement carries no information. Independent authorities raise expected confidence in sound rules and lower it for unsound ones.

4/5 Coordinated control of several authorities lowers the chance an error is disclosed below what independent control reaches at the same average independence. Academia, religion, media, schools and medical bodies are compared across six regime types.

5/5 The ideology changes. The institutional functions remain.
