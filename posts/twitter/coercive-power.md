1/5 Who enforces the rules? Part V of the Beast System series starts from one axiom: members sometimes have reason to break rules. A deterrence theorem follows.

2/5 When the share of agents tempted to violate exceeds the violation rate the collective can tolerate, compliance requires that violators' continuation payoffs fall because of their own conduct. Some sanction regime is necessary. No particular institution is.

3/5 Gossip, ostracism and coalitions against would-be dominants meet that requirement as well as police do, as ethnography of the Nuer, the Ju/'hoansi and Ostrom's commons shows.

4/5 Any enforcement apparatus has two margins: conduct (violators vs compliers sanctioned) and allegiance (opponents vs supporters sanctioned). They vary independently, so necessity of coercion does not imply capture. Spending and arrest counts mismeasure coercive power.

5/5 The ideology changes. The institutional functions remain.
