1/5 Who produces what the system consumes? Part VI of the Beast System series treats production, payment and social reproduction as the sixth pillar. From a maintenance axiom, a persistent collective must produce at a positive rate and reproduce its producers through care labor.

2/5 Rents, tribute, migration or automation relocate production. They do not remove it.

3/5 That dependence gives producers a latent veto: if outside revenue falls short of what institutions cost to maintain, collective withdrawal of labor, taxes or trade threatens their persistence.

4/5 A bargaining model makes it exact. Producers' terms rise with their outside option and organized weight, fall linearly with concentration of control over productive participation, and lose the withdrawal premium once rents cover maintenance.

5/5 Every tool that depresses producers' terms sits in another pillar, so capture of production never starts inside it. The ideology changes. The institutional functions remain.
