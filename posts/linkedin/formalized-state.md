The six-pillar framework identifies an invariant structure of human governance, supported by historical observation and given precise expression through mathematics. Part VII of the Beast System series, the capstone, defines that structure formally.

Every governance system decides who controls resources, makes and interprets rules, shapes beliefs, enforces and produces. The Formalized State is a class of structures with six layers of control, a role map and a dependency hypergraph. Four results are proved relative to stated axioms and a stated set of preserved properties: roles, control shares, concentration, contestability, integration and dependencies.

Representation: every governance system satisfying the axioms is equivalent to a Formalized State structure, with content, ideology and procedure forgotten. Necessity: six axioms about collective life (scarcity, plurality, generality, coordination, defection and maintenance) force the six functions, each axiom can fail in a persistent model, and four incidence axioms make the functions fully interdependent, although interpretation needs legitimation or coercion rather than both. Minimality: dropping or merging any pillar identifies systems that differ in a preserved property, with explicit witness pairs. Uniqueness: the abstraction is the coarsest one preserving those properties, unique up to unique isomorphism, with exactly six dimensions.

The central point is that structure is universal while capture is contingent. Structures satisfying every axiom occupy all four corners of the concentration and integration square, so having the structure does not imply capture. Encodings of Aristotle, Weber, Parsons, Easton, Almond, Luhmann and Mann each identify two systems the abstraction distinguishes, so none is equivalent to it. The regime analyses draw on the historical and ethnographic record, and the indices are defined so real polities can be scored. Capitalist, communist party-state, democratic socialist, Islamic, historical Catholic monarchical and secular democratic systems are treated evenly. 'Beast System' is a typological reading of Revelation 13 used as a name for a structure, never for a religion, people, regime, country, party or person.

The ideology changes. The institutional functions remain.
